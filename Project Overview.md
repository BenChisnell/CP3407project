CP3407 Advanced Software Engineering Project Assignment Website development and creation of “FeedMe”.

Project Overview

Develop a modern, user-friendly website application for “FeedMe”, an innovative food delivery platform service designed to transform the way customers discover, order and receive meals. The platform will be free for end-users (customers) and monetized through partnerships with restaurants, delivery service fees, and premium business solutions.


Project Brief

Target audience

•	Service Providers
Restaurants, cafes and kitchens seeking to expand their digital present and increased sales.
Delivery partners responsible for transporting orders efficiently and reliably.

•	End users
Individuals and families looking for convenient, fast and reliable meal delivery (and pickup services).
Busy professionals, students and households seeking diverse dining options at their fingertips.
