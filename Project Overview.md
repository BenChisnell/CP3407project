CP3407 Advanced Software Engineering Project Assignment Website development and creation of “FeedMe”.

Project Overview

Develop a modern, user-friendly website application for “FeedMe”, an innovative food delivery platform service designed to transform the way customers discover, order and receive meals. The platform will be free for end-users (customers) and monetized through partnerships with restaurants, delivery service fees, and premium business solutions.


Project Brief

Target audience

•	Service Providers
Restaurants, cafes and kitchens seeking to expand their digital present and increased sales.
Delivery partners responsible for transporting orders efficiently and reliably.

•	End users
Individuals and families looking for convenient, fast and reliable meal delivery (and pickup services).
Busy professionals, students and households seeking diverse dining options at their fingertips.



Primary Goals
For Service Providers:
•	Enable real-time updates for pricing, availability and promotions.
•	Streamline order fulfilment, payment processing and customer communications

For End Users:
•	Deliver an intuitive and seamless interface for browsing restaurants and placing orders.
•	Allow customers to track deliveries in real-time and receive notifications.
•	Provide secure payment options.
•	Enable easy communication with restaurants and delivery personnel.
•	Offer features such as order history, ratings, reviews and food type searches (ie. Gluten-free)



Unique Selling Position:

To differentiate FeedMe from other food delivery services, the platform will incorporate innovative and value-driven features, including:
•	Local Business Support: Prioritizing local business support closest to users’ location where possible.
•	Transparent Pricing: Clear breakdown of delivery fees with no hidden charges (delivery fees, GST, fuel levies, etc)
•	Real-time Order Tracking: Real-time notifications via sms
•	Integrated Reviews and Ratings: Ensuring quality assurance and trust among users.
