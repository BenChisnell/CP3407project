## Project Overview

Develop a modern, user-friendly website application for “FeedMe”, an innovative food delivery platform service designed to transform the way customers discover, order and receive meals.  

The platform will be free for end-users (customers) and monetized through:
- Partnerships with restaurants  
- Delivery service fees

## Target Audience

#### Service Providers
- Restaurants, cafes and kitchens seeking to expand their digital present and increased sales  
- Delivery partners responsible for transporting orders efficiently and reliably  

#### End Users
- Individuals and families looking for convenient, fast and reliable meal delivery (and pickup services)  
- Busy professionals, students and households seeking diverse dining options at their fingertips  

---

## Primary Goals

### For Service Providers
- Enable real-time updates for pricing, availability and promotions  
- Streamline order fulfilment, payment processing and customer communications  

### For End Users
- Deliver an intuitive and seamless interface for browsing restaurants and placing orders  
- Allow customers to track deliveries in real-time and receive notifications  
- Provide secure payment options  
- Enable easy communication with restaurants and delivery personnel  
- Offer features such as:
  - Order history  
  - Ratings   
  - Food type searches (e.g., gluten-free)  

---

## Unique Selling Position

To differentiate FeedMe from other food delivery services, the platform will incorporate innovative and value-driven features, including:

- **Local Business Support**  
  Prioritizing local business support closest to users’ location where possible  

- **Transparent Pricing**  
  Clear breakdown of delivery fees with no hidden charges (delivery fees, GST, fuel levies, etc)  

- **Real-time Order Tracking**  
  Real-time notifications via SMS  

- **Integrated Reviews and Ratings**  
  Ensuring quality assurance and trust among users
