
# User story title: Filter restaurants by cuisine

## Priority: 40
Priority is a 40 because filtering by cuisine improves user convenience and helps users quickly find preferred food types, but it is not critical to core functionality since users can still browse or search for restaurants without it.

## Estimation: 4 days
* Piper: 4 day

## Assumptions:
* Users have specific cuisine preferences and use them as a primary way to decide what to eat.
* Restaurants in the app are accurately categorised by cuisine, with consistent tagging across listings.

## Description: 
As a user of the app, Amelia a Female aged 28 wants to filter restaurants by cuisine so that she can quickly narrow down options and find food that matches her preferences or cravings without browsing through irrelevant choices.
