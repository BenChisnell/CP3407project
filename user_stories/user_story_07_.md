
# User story title: Search for Gluten-Free Only Food

## Priority: 20
Priority is a 20 because this feature is critical for users with dietary restrictions or conditions, where consuming gluten can cause serious health issues. Ensuring these users can safely identify suitable food options is essential for accessibility, safety, and trust in the app.

## Description: 
As a user of the app, Leo a Male aged 40 wants to search for only gluten-free food options so that he can safely choose meals that meet my dietary needs and avoid any risk of getting sick from consuming gluten.
