
# User story title: Pay with debit or credit on delivery

## Priority: 10
Priority is a 10 because enabling users to pay with debit or credit card on delivery is a core payment function, allowing them to complete transactions conveniently and securely. Without a reliable payment option, users may be unable or unwilling to place orders, making it essential to the app’s operation.

## Assumptions:
* Delivery personnel have access to secure card payment devices (e.g., EFTPOS) to process payments at the time of delivery.
* The app clearly indicates that card-on-delivery is available. 

## Description: 
As a user of the app, Ethan a male aged 32 wants to pay with a debit or credit card on delivery so that he can complete his purchase conveniently at the time he receives his order without needing to pay in advance.
