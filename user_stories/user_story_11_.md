
# User story title: Enter a delivery address

## Priority: 10
Priority is a 10 because entering a delivery address is essential for order fulfilment, ensuring that food is delivered to the correct location. Without this information, orders cannot be completed or delivered, making it a critical core feature of the app.

As a user of the app, Ava a femae aged 29 wants to enter her delivery address so that her order is delivered to the correct location without delays or errors.
