
# User story title: View order history

## Priority: 40
Priority is a 40 because viewing order history improves convenience by allowing users to quickly reorder past meals, but it is not essential to the core functionality of browsing, ordering, and completing a purchase.

## Estimation: 2 days 
* Piper: 3 day
* Terry: 2 days
* Ben: 1 days
* Lachlan: 2 day
* Jack: 1 days

## Assumptions:
* Users can access their order history only when logged into their account.
* Order history data is presented clearly and remains available over time without being lost.

## Description: 
As a user of the app, Benjamin a male aged 37 wants to view his order history so that he can quickly reorder previous meals and keep track of his past purchases.
